--- title: "LawLM: Intelligent Legal-Contract Review" excerpt: "A large language model application for legal-document processing and contract review." collection: portfolio ---

Role: Technical development and testing
Period: Oct. 2023 – Feb. 2024
Organizations: Wuhan University and Zhongnan University of Economics and Law

LawLM is a large language model system for intelligent legal-contract review. I managed the end-to-end workflow for legal-document data acquisition, cleaning, and annotation, then evaluated OCR-based contract parsing for the system.

I also contributed to an integrated user-interface system associated with a software copyright registration.
