**Role:** Large language model algorithm design and training<br>
**Period:** Mar. 2025 – Jun. 2026<br>
**Organizations:** Wuhan University and Tencent Cloud

SkyThink is a multimodal large language model for remote-sensing education. I planned and processed a dataset that covers the core undergraduate remote-sensing curriculum and contains more than one million text and multimodal entries.

I developed a multi-task adaptive framework to address task conflicts, using gradient adjustment to improve interaction and robustness across training datasets. I also designed a benchmark for remote-sensing education with curriculum-wide coverage and fine-tuned the foundation model with LoRA for domain-specific tasks.

Selected SkyThink and SkySense capabilities were integrated into SkySage, an instructional remote-sensing product developed with Tencent Cloud and Tencent Education.
