**Role:** Large language model algorithm design and training<br>
**Period:** Mar. 2025 – Jun. 2026<br>
**Organizations:** Wuhan University and Tencent Cloud

SkyThink is an end-to-end multimodal foundation-model project for remote-sensing education. I planned and processed a million-scale dataset spanning core undergraduate RS courses, with text, visual-language, and mathematical-language examples.

I developed a collaborative multi-task adaptive framework to address task conflicts, using gradient modulation to strengthen cross-task interaction and robustness. I also designed a curriculum-wide RS education benchmark and fine-tuned a foundation model with LoRA for domain-specific multimodal tasks.

Selected SkyThink and SkySense capabilities were integrated into SkySage, an instructional remote-sensing product developed with Tencent Cloud and Tencent Education. This work reflects my interest in scalable multimodal systems that connect perception, reasoning, and real-world educational use.
