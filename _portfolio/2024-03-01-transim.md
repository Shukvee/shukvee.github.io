**Role:** Vision-system design and development<br>
**Period:** Mar. 2024 – Jun. 2024<br>
**Organization:** Wuhan University

Transim is a multi-source traffic-simulation model. I designed and developed a YOLO-based computer-vision system to recognize and count vehicles and pedestrians, measure traffic flow, and identify road and traffic anomalies.

I also helped improve signal-acquisition equipment to enhance stability and generality. The resulting invention received a patent.
