<p><strong>Role:</strong> Technical development and testing<br />
<strong>Period:</strong> Oct. 2023 – Feb. 2024<br />
<strong>Organizations:</strong> Wuhan University and Zhongnan University of Economics and Law</p>

<p>LawLM is a large language model system for intelligent legal-contract review. I managed the end-to-end workflow for legal-document data acquisition, cleaning, and annotation, then evaluated OCR-based contract parsing for the system.</p>

<p>I also contributed to an integrated user-interface system associated with a software copyright registration.</p>
