Selected projects

* Designed and prepared a comprehensive dataset spanning undergraduate remote-sensing courses, with more than one million text and multimodal entries.

* Managed the full workflow for legal-document data acquisition, cleaning, and annotation.

**Transim: Multi-source traffic-simulation model**, Wuhan University, Mar. 2024 - Jun. 2024

* Designed a vision system using YOLO to count vehicles and pedestrians, measure traffic flow, and identify road and traffic anomalies.
* Improved signal-acquisition equipment to increase stability and generality; the related invention received a patent.

**Smart Patrol Line: Autonomous power-line monitoring system**, Wuhan University, Sep. 2024 - Feb. 2025

* Applied DeepLSD to detect power lines during flight and used an improved simulated-annealing algorithm for inspection-route planning.
* Assisted with a voxel-Transformer point-cloud classification model and developed mathematical models for power-line fitting with and without wind deflection.
